Mix any head, body and legs

100 animals. Lion head, chicken body, cheetah legs? Sure. Each part brings its own moves and sounds.

Pick from all 100. Press TAB and click, or just type a name like "giraffe".

Add-ons. 12 per part: top hats, capes, jetpacks, stilts, rocket boots… or Hurt mode.

Real fur. Furry animals get layered hair, shaggy manes and lighter bellies.

10 big maps. Farm, Savanna, Arctic, Jungle, Desert, Beach, Forest, Mountains, Swamp and City Park.

Knock stuff over. Crates, snowmen, bowling pins, dominoes and red barrels that go boom.

Controls

W A S DMove (drag the mouse to look around)
SHIFTRun, scurry or gallop
SPACEJump. With wings: press again to flap, hold to glide
1 2 3Change head, body, legs (SHIFT goes back)
4 5 6Change the add-on on each part
TABAll 100 animals (type to search)
ZRandom morph
E F R QBody move, voice, eat, legs move (shown on screen)
B · J · ESCMaps · Stats · Pause

Opening it the first time

Unzip it, then right-click "Morph Simulator" and choose Open, then Open again. macOS asks this once because the game isn't from the App Store.
